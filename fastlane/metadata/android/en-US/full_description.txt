Hausmusik controls your Sonos speakers directly in your home network, without a cloud account. It also works with older S1 systems.

- Rooms and groups: group by dragging, one volume per room, mute single rooms
- Stage: the playing track in the colours of its cover
- Queue: reorder, remove with undo, play next, save as Sonos playlist
- Library: favourites, music share, artists, albums, genres, radio, line-in
- Linked music services such as Spotify and bonob: browse and search
- Alarms, sleep timer, sound, crossfade
- Notification, lock screen and volume buttons, widget, quick settings tile, system output switcher
- Optional: synced lyrics, artist pictures and background from MusicBrainz and Wikipedia

No ads, no tracking, no account. Hausmusik is free software under the GNU GPL 3.0 or later; the source is at github.com/linuxundich/hausmusik.
